About Us

Who we are

SAI Process Sensing For Oil & Gas Fields Equipment L.L.C is a UAE-based supplier of advanced industrial instrumentation and process sensing solutions for the oil & gas, water treatment, power, manufacturing, and process industries.

We provide safe, reliable, and innovative solutions for pressure, differential pressure, level, temperature, and flow measurement applications through trusted global partners and manufacturers.

Our focus is on delivering high-performance instrumentation, technical support, and responsive service to help customers improve operational efficiency, safety, and process reliability across demanding industrial environments.
